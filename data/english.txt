Basic Information:
Sandip University is located in Mahiravani, Trimbak Road, Nashik, Maharashtra.
It was established in 2017.
It is a private university approved by UGC and Maharashtra Government.
It has NAAC A Grade accreditation.
Campus size is approximately 250+ acres.

Campus Overview:
The campus is a large green area of 250+ acres with modern infrastructure.
The campus is fully WiFi enabled.
It has separate academic, administrative, and hostel buildings.

Academic Facilities:
The university provides smart classrooms with projectors.
There are advanced computer labs and engineering labs.
Law students have access to a moot court.
The library is well-stocked with digital resources.

Hostel:
Separate hostels are available for boys and girls.
Rooms are WiFi enabled.
Mess and canteen facilities are available.
24x7 medical support is provided.

Sports:
Indoor sports include chess, carrom, and badminton.
Outdoor sports include basketball, athletics, and archery.
Gym and fitness facilities are available.
